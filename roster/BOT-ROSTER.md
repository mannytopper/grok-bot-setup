## How to read this roster

- All times are in America/New_York.
- Most routines are housed and run by the Routines bot on behalf of the bot whose job they serve. Those rows are marked “run by Routines.” Connor's shared calendar sync still runs on Connor. Volt's own weekly usage review, if it is ever turned on, stays with Volt.
- Nothing runs or reaches Manny on Shabbat or Yom Tov.
- Enabled and paused states are taken from the Routines bot's move log and prompt files (current through November 23, 2026) and from Connor's current brief (November 20, 2026). A live routine list from the app was not available in this session, so a routine could have been changed in the app after those notes were written.
- Skills in the shared library and Cursor-managed skills are available to every bot. They are listed once at the end, with a one-line description each. Under a bot, only the names of key skills that bot clearly uses or was written for are listed, without repeating those descriptions.
- Personal email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, and account numbers are left out of this roster.
- Retired bots with no current profile are not listed. That includes Usage Guard (folded into Volt), Checkup, Call List Remover, Bot Optomizer, and earlier October retirements.
